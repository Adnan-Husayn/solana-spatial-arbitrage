Which side is dear

One venue usually prices SOL a little higher than the other. In principle you sell where it is dear and buy back where it is cheap.

Why the hurdle matters

A round trip pays both pools their fees. Past that, a Jito tip, a transaction fee and price impact still have to be covered.

Why it rarely opens

This is one of the most watched pairs on Solana. Searchers with faster data close any gap long before a page polling a public endpoint could see it.
