How to read this

  • Spread is the gap between the two spot prices. It is gross: no fees are taken out.
  • Fee hurdle is what a round trip pays the two pools. A spread below it cannot be profitable at any size.
  • Net result also subtracts a Jito tip of 10,000 lamports and a 25,000 lamport transaction fee, and accounts for price impact.
  • Orca quotes are only trusted inside the pool's current tick range, so large trades are not estimated.
  • Raydium's price uses vault balances, which sit slightly off the pool's true reserves.
  • Data comes from a public RPC by polling every few seconds. A real bot sees updates sooner and still loses most races on this pair.
